### 4. Visualizing Geometric Optimization: From 9th-Century Baghdad to Modern AI

**Mathematical History · Computational Visualization · Optimization**

An independent computational study exploring connections between classical mathematical reasoning and optimization concepts used in modern machine learning.

The project investigates historical mathematical ideas associated with **al-Khwarizmi** and visualizes their relationship with contemporary optimization concepts such as loss surfaces and gradient-based minimization.

**Technologies:** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib
